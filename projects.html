EcoVision AI

A waste-classification website that helps people sort waste more thoughtfully.

Improper waste segregation makes recycling harder and adds to pollution. EcoVision AI lets people upload an image or use a camera to identify a waste category and get disposal guidance. I worked on feeding and training the image model with Google Teachable Machine.

ScamShieldAI

A team-built website that helps people spot suspicious messages and scam websites.

Fraudulent messages can pressure people into sharing sensitive information or opening unsafe links. ScamShieldAI brings scam-awareness checks into a web experience. I contributed to the HTML part of the project; the public demo is hosted by a teammate.
